    How we compute

    ratio = Σ locker balances × price ÷ (Σ account value + staking rewards + referral rewards)

    Reserves: balances read directly from the lockers (Ethereum eth_call, Solana RPC, Cardano Koios/Blockfrost); ADA/ETH/SOL priced with Strike's /price/v2/premiumIndex index price, fiat-redeemable stablecoins at their 1:1 peg (labelled), anything else listed as unpriced — never guessed.
    Liabilities: every account from /stat/v1/stats/leaderboard read live via /v2/account (margin_balance) and /v2/balances (stakingRewardBalance), sub-accounts included, vaults excluded, ≤ 40 requests/s.
    Status: Balanced within ±0.01%, Drift up to ±1%, Needs review beyond (never published as insolvency). Gaps explained by components Strike does not expose yet show as Incomplete. Read-only, open source (MIT): GitHub.
