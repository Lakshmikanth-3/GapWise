Strike isn't a bank: it should hold exactly what it owes. Over 100% is as suspicious as under, so ZeroGap aims for a gap of zero.

  • Every account counted: margin balances, depositors' vault shares and staking rewards from Strike's public API.
  • Every locker read: on-chain balances priced with one snapshot of Strike's own feeds.
  • Check your own funds: connect with Strike Builder Connect. You sign; ZeroGap never holds keys.
  • Honest about gaps: every number shows its source, time and confidence, and open questions are published.

How it works

01

Read the lockers

Balances straight from Strike's contracts on Ethereum, Solana and both Cardano scripts, priced in USD.

02

Sum every account

About 3,500 accounts and sub-accounts read live from Strike's API, plus vault shares and rewards owed.

03

Publish and alert

The ratio, a signed JSON report and hourly history are published; any gap or safety change reaches Telegram.
